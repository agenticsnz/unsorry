Adds `tools.intake.remigrate_benchmark_subs` — a tested, reusable, dry-run-by-default tool (`--suite`/`--parent` scoping) for the one-time ADR-116 clean-up: it discovers benchmark decomposition sub-lemmas that were proved at the *repo* pin before ADR-116 (and so cannot be used by their parents' suite-pinned recompose) and re-pins them — re-opening the goal record and removing the repo-pin module, its `library/index` artifact, and its `proved`-outcome `proof-runs` records — so the swarm re-proves them at the suite pin.

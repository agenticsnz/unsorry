An ADR-016 infrastructure abort (the provider CLI died fast and the health probe also failed) now **surfaces why**: before the prove worktree — and its `prove-attempt-<n>.log` — is removed, the harness copies that log to `$UNSORRY_WORKDIR/last-infra-failure.log` and echoes its last 20 lines to the console. Previously the operator saw only `infrastructure failure … (ADR-016)` with no visibility into the CLI's actual error (auth/quota/network), because `prove_goal` deletes the worktree on every exit. A missing/empty log degrades gracefully with a clear note.

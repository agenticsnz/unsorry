**Gate A now enforces the ADR-011 statement binding for proofs made at a suite pin (#7191).** ADR-011 exists so that Gate A — not the contributor — decides what "proved this statement" means, and `check_in_proof` deletes the proving agent's own binding before opening the PR precisely because Gate A is expected to regenerate it authoritatively. That regeneration never happened for a benchmark goal: `check_statement_binding` enumerated only `library/index`, so a proof staged into `targets/<suite>/_verify/library/` was skipped, and `gate-a-benchmark` (a `lake build --wfail` plus a forbidden-token scan) has no binding step of its own. Statement identity for every suite-pinned proof was therefore verified on the proving agent's machine and nowhere else — a proof of a weakened or vacuous restatement under the goal's name would have passed Gate A, which is the exact failure class ADR-011 was written against. The generator now enumerates every proof library and writes each binding beside the module it binds, and `gate-a-benchmark` regenerates them before its build. Measured on the repo: bindings went from 386 to 394, and all 8 suite bindings build clean at the minif2f-v1 pin — retroactively confirming that the proofs already on `main` do inhabit their goal types. This is the fifth appearance of the single-`library/index` assumption after #7158, #7166 and #7189, and the first with soundness rather than cosmetic consequences.

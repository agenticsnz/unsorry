Added ADR-078 (Draft) — Structural (Package-Graph) Non-Triviality and Scoring. Records the reframe (from Leo / khive AI) that a goal's hardness is structural, not intrinsic: a lemma is non-trivial when something larger that someone architected depends on it. Proposes scoring goals by depth + fan-in in a curated package's decomposition DAG instead of a standalone tactic battery — farm-resistant (you cannot manufacture sub-lemmas of a theorem you did not architect) and valuable (discharging an obligation yields a verified artifact, not points). Draft for discussion; open questions cover the curated-target trust layer, cross-prover/domain reach (the Lion pilot), the exact formula, and merge policy.

Added ADR-079 (Draft) — Platform Generalisation and the Self-Verification Gating Invariant. Companion to ADR-078. Anchored in the founding plan (distributed-research-swarm-plan.md), it makes explicit that the swarm is a general engine for any domain carrying a cheap, deterministic, kernel-grade self-verifier (math, formal software/hardware verification like Lion, constructions with exact certificates) — and that domains without such a verifier (soft-scored or physically-validated, founding ranks 4–9) are out of scope for the trustless commons because they would make it poisonable. Records the commons-not-contractor governance principle for curated targets. Draft for founder ratification (mission scope).

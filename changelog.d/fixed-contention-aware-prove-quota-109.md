**Contention-aware prove-PR quota + un-strand quota-closed branches (ADR-109 / SPEC-109-A, amends ADR-054).** Fixes a deadlock that stalled a sole contributor's queue (e.g. `@ohdearquant`'s ~117 template proofs) for days with the gate idle and nobody else waiting: the per-author quota was a flat 20 while the dispatcher's submission governor opens up to 40, so PRs 21–40 got quota-closed — and the dispatcher then treated those *closed* PRs as "already exists" (`--state all`) and never re-dispatched them. Now the per-author cap is **fair-share = `UNSORRY_MAX_OPEN_PROVE_PRS` ÷ active contributors** (`pr_admission.fair_share_cap`): a lone contributor gets the whole budget (no spurious closes), N contributors get equal shares (ADR-054 fairness preserved). And the dispatcher (`queued_branch_has_pr`) now treats an `over-author-quota`-closed branch as **retryable** past a back-off (`UNSORRY_QUOTA_RETRY_BACKOFF_S`, default 3600 s), so a transient quota close no longer strands a branch forever — making the quota's "the goal returns to the pool" promise real. Churn-safe (sole contributor → no closes; back-off bounds retries under contention). Refs #6751.

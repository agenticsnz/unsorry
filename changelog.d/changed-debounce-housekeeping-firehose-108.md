**Debounce the post-merge housekeeping refresh firehose (ADR-108 / SPEC-108-A).** The five `push`-triggered refreshers (`leaderboard`, `proofs-visualisation`, `targets-board`, `adr-index`, `attribution-relabel`) — ~66% of commits to `main` (#6751 §7 B1) — now skip a refresh that already landed within a tunable window (`UNSORRY_REFRESH_DEBOUNCE_SECONDS`, default 600 s) via the unit-tested `tools/repo/refresh_debounce.py` guard. `push` stays primary (first merge after a quiet period refreshes immediately), so this relieves the merge surface, the central `REFRESH_TOKEN` PAT budget, and the `cancel-in-progress` churn *without* the cron throttling (~1×/hr) that left the board 1–2 h stale (#3720). Safe against the leaderboard freshness gate (ADR-098): the debounce bounds a board's source-relative lag to ≤ the window, well under the 30-min threshold. `schedule`/`workflow_dispatch` runs are never debounced; the three refreshers that lacked a schedule gain a sparse `*/15` cron flush-backstop. Reversible via `UNSORRY_REFRESH_DEBOUNCE_SECONDS=0`. Refs #6751.

A batch (ADR-107 `prove-batch(…)`) whose Gate A failed on a transient **infrastructure flake** — a full-`.lake`-volume `ENOSPC` on a `replay` shard that landed cold and died decompressing mathlib — is now **re-run instead of split into singletons**. Previously the batch-recovery-janitor treated any non-cancellation failure as genuine and redispatched every constituent as its own PR, so one runner's disk-full moment discarded up to 8 amortised proofs and re-verified each from scratch (observed: batch #7109). The janitor now detects the disk-full signature in the failed step logs and reruns the batch **bounded by `run_attempt`** — after the attempt cap a persistently-full volume still isolates the constituents so nothing strands. Genuine per-proof failures redispatch exactly as before (the new path is gated on the infra-flake signal, default off).

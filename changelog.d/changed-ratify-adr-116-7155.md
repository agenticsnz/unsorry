**ADR-116 (Suite-Aware Decomposition) is ratified `Accepted` on the strength of a completed pilot (#7155).** The implementation shipped in #7129 but the record still read `Proposed` behind a design-only banner saying no behaviour shipped. It has now closed its motivating case end to end: `aime-1983-p9` and all five of its sub-lemmas are `proved` on `main`, kernel-verified at the minif2f-v1 pin, with `skeleton.aisp` byte-identical throughout. A new Pilot Outcome section records what was confirmed — descendant resolution works transitively to depth 2, and `gate-a-benchmark` really does verify the whole suite library rather than only registered obligations, which the ADR had asserted as "confirmed during implementation" without it ever having been observed — and what the pilot corrected. The main correction: the design specified the prove and recompose paths but not the *readers* of proof state, so Gate B (#7158) and the harness's unblock/recompose sweep (#7166) each had to learn about suite indices independently, discovered one production failure at a time. A second index namespace is a cross-cutting change, not a localised one. Also recorded: ADR-115's assembly prompt is what closed both recomposes, while its retry ladder never fired and remains untested.

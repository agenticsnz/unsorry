**The targets board marks suite-pinned proofs as proved, and the upstream pipeline still won't offer them to mathlib.** `tools.sourcing.targets_board._proved` enumerated the repo library and archive blocks only, so a benchmark obligation proved at its suite's pin (ADR-099/ADR-116) never showed as proved on `docs/targets.md` — even though `tools.leaderboard.registered_targets._proved_goal_ids`, whose docstring says it *mirrors that function*, had already been made suite-aware. The two had silently diverged. Fixing it alone would have caused a second, worse bug: `tools.upstream.eligible` consumes that same set, and a benchmark obligation satisfies every other packet criterion because it carries a `backlog/<id>.md` with an `Absence:` field like any sourced goal. Measured, the naive fix took upstream eligibility from **0 to 3** — offering `aime-1983-p9`, `amc12a-2003-p24` and `amc12a-2008-p15`, all competition problem statements, as mathlib contributions. `eligible` now excludes the suite namespace explicitly. Both call sites go through a new `tools/proof_index.py`, the first shared answer to "where do proofs live"; five readers previously hand-rolled that enumeration and the suite namespace was the one they forgot, five times over (#7158, #7166, #7189, #7191, and this). The module documents which readers are repo-scoped **by design** — `tools.archive.plan` must stay repo-only, because archiving moves a module into a new package with its own toolchain and a suite proof already lives in one pinned to its suite's Lean+mathlib.

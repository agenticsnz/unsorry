The local queue dispatcher (`swarm/agent.sh --dispatch-queue`, as launched by `run.sh`) now fetches `queued/prove/*` with `--prune`, so a branch the hourly stale-branch janitor deletes on the remote also drops its local remote-tracking ref. Without it, a long-lived dispatcher accumulated stale `refs/remotes/origin/queued/prove/*` refs (observed: 188 local vs 1 actually on the remote) and re-attempted `gh pr create` against the deleted branches every pass — the `Head ref must be a branch` / `No commits between main` failures and `already proved on main` skips an operator sees as the swarm "looping on dispatch". The scheduled backstop dispatcher was never affected (fresh checkout per run); only a local `run.sh` loop leaked. Prune is scoped to the queued/prove refspec, so `origin/main` and other tracking refs are untouched.
